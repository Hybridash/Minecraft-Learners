# 7. Stay safe when downloading mods

Mods are programs. A bad one can steal accounts or damage your computer, so be picky about where they come from.

## Do

- Download mods and modpacks **through Prism** or from **[modrinth.com](https://modrinth.com)** and **[curseforge.com](https://www.curseforge.com)**.
- Get OptiFine only from **optifine.net**.
- Check that a mod has lots of downloads, recent updates and a real author page.
- Keep backups of worlds you care about. In **Edit → Worlds** you can copy a world, or copy the instance's `saves` folder.

## Don't

- Download mods from random "mod download" sites, YouTube descriptions, or links sent in DMs.
- Run `.exe` files that claim to be mods. Minecraft Java mods are `.jar` files, and you never need to double-click them.
- Use "cracked" launchers or clients. They're a common way to spread malware.
- Give your Microsoft password to anything except Microsoft's own sign-in page.

## If something seems wrong

- Delete the suspicious mod, change your Microsoft password, and turn on two-step verification at [account.microsoft.com](https://account.microsoft.com).
- Run a virus scan.
