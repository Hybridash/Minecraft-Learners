# 5. Install a whole modpack

⏱️ About 5–15 minutes, depending on the pack.

A **modpack** is a ready-made bundle of mods that someone already set up to work together. It's the easiest way to play with lots of mods.

## Steps

1. In the main window, click **Add Instance** (top-left).
2. The **New Instance** window opens. In the list on the **left**, click where the pack is from:
   - **Modrinth**
   - **CurseForge**
   - **ATLauncher**, **FTB Legacy**, **Technic** (less common)

   (Look at the [New Instance picture](../images/new-instance.svg): these are the items under **Custom** on the left.)
3. Type the pack's name in the **search box** at the top, e.g. `Fabulously Optimized` or `All the Mods`.
4. Click the pack in the list. Its description appears.
5. At the bottom, there's a **version** list or dropdown. Leave it on the **newest** version (the top one) unless a friend or server tells you to use a specific one.
6. Click **OK** in the bottom-right corner.
7. Prism downloads the pack. A progress window shows up. Wait until it closes.
   - 🚫 If a **"Please download the missing mods"** window appears, see [Add mods → CurseForge](04-add-mods.md#-please-download-the-missing-mods-curseforge-only).
8. The pack shows up as a new instance in the main window. Click it **once**, then click **Launch**.

✅ **Done when:** the modpack's title screen appears. Big packs can take **several minutes** to start the first time. That's normal.

## I have a modpack file (.zip or .mrpack)

1. Click **Add Instance**.
2. On the left, click **Import**.
3. Click **Browse**, pick the `.zip` or `.mrpack` file, and click **OK**.

## Tips

- 💾 **Big packs need more memory**, or they crash with *"OutOfMemoryError"*. See [Fix problems → Not enough memory](06-troubleshooting.md#not-enough-memory).
- 👫 Playing with friends? Everyone needs the **same pack and the same version**, and so does the server.
